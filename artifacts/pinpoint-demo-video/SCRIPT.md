# Pinpoint Desk OS — 90-second demo film

Format 16:9 · English · 90.0 s · 12 shots · music only (no voiceover, no SFX).

## Treatment

The posting, answered screen by screen. The film opens on Pinpoint's own words (the three leaks: data entry, duplicate records, scattered emails), then shows the proof of concept answering each use case the posting names, one chapter per use case, with a slate that always reads "From the posting: …" on the left and the Desk OS screen that answers it on the right. Order follows the demo's own guided tour: Tuesday 08:12, a mandate arrives; follow-ups; the longlist funnel; two strong matches set aside; a CV that tried to give an order; the record; search and voice; the refusal and the log; the test on closed mandates; the ninety-day pilot.

Art direction: maroon corporate-editorial. Pinpoint's deep maroon (#420008), crimson (#AC292E), rose (#F1B8BB) and warm paper (#F7F4F4); Instrument Sans throughout (the product's own typeface, loaded locally), JetBrains Mono for record codes and log lines, Assistant Light only inside the product's header lockup. Two alternating media: full-bleed kinetic numerals and type on maroon, and the product's own interface rebuilt as non-interactive artwork on paper (cards, tiles, tables, pills at macro scale). The carrier is the crimson pin-dot from the Pinpoint mark: it lands on what matters and opens as an aperture into the next chapter (three times: hook → product, CV → record, day 90 → close). Other cuts are motivated wipes, pushes and one hard cut on "Refused." Every cut sits on a bar line of the bed (96 BPM, bars every 2.5 s); internal beats sit on the 0.625 s grid and the strong beat-4 hits (bar start − 0.6 s) carry stamps and hand-offs.

Honesty rules that the film keeps on screen: concept and synthetic data label on every chapter slate; registers "simulated"; Microsoft 365, Outlook and Microdec "not connected in this demo"; nothing is sent; voice is Gemini Live, read-only, audio sent to Google only after consent; independent concept by Roland-Caryl Obam Ngo'O, not a Pinpoint product. No price, no claim, no figure that is not in the demo, the dossier or the deck. The official Pinpoint mark and wordmark are used as supplied PNGs, never redrawn. No button, link or call to action inside the composition.

## Sources for every on-screen fact

- Posting (Oxford CareerConnect): "our recruiters lose hours every week to data entry, duplicate records and scattered emails"; "We think AI can fix that, and we want a brilliant graduate to prove it"; "clean and organise our data"; "candidate matching, smart search, CV parsing and follow-up suggestions"; "Run a live pilot … week by week"; "an opinion on where they're useful and where they aren't".
- Demo (artifacts/pinpoint-desk-os, read only): all figures, codes, labels and UI copy.
- Deck and dossier: guided-tour order, the three top profiles with their dated reasons, "8 of 10 admission years confirmed", "5 of 10 last checked over a year ago", "Approved to call · by L-02 · 08:31", the ninety-day plan and stop rule, "the first layer of Future Recruiter AI".
